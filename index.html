About Us

A Legacy of Discipline & Long-Term Thinking

Fraser Heritage Holdings was founded on the belief that exceptional families can achieve more together than alone. We operate as a private investment office, combining deep expertise in alternative assets with sophisticated portfolio construction and family office services.

Our approach draws on Endowment and All-Weather principles — deeply diversified and carefully constructed to compound through cycles, not just survive them. We invest alongside our partners, applying the same rigor to every allocation.

Our Philosophy

Principles That Guide Every Decision

Our investment philosophy is rooted in patience, discipline, and a commitment to preserving capital while seeking asymmetric opportunities.

Long-Term Alignment

We design partnerships with generational time horizons. Our culture rewards long-term judgment over short-term convenience, ensuring every decision serves the family's enduring interests.

Deep Diversification

Our portfolios draw on Endowment and All-Weather principles — carefully constructed to compound through market cycles, not merely survive them. True resilience comes from thoughtful construction.

Co-Investment

Investing alongside our partners has been a defining principle since inception. We apply the same approach to our own capital that we apply to every family we serve — skin in the game, always.
